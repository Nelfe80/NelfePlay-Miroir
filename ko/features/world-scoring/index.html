What is World Scoring?

World Scoring is the worldwide ranking of retro game scores played at home. You launch a game from RetroBat's NELFEPLAY WORLD SCORING collection; the machine applies the reference settings, measures the game, and sends the score with a signed passport.

Each leaderboard is a challenge: a single credit (1CC), a single life (1LC), together, or per game mode. The site checks every score before publishing it.

The story

A ranked game

A game counts when it is launched from the NELFEPLAY WORLD SCORING collection: the same game launched from its system stays a free game, with no score or replay. The machine applies the reference settings, turns off rewind, run-ahead and auto-save for this game, and says before the game whether it will be certifiable. In arcade, FBNeo is imposed first.

The challenges

1CC: the score made on a single credit, game finished or not; ✓1CC requires finishing the game. 1LC: the score of the first life; the game goes on afterwards. 1CC MULTI and 1LC MULTI: the same challenges together. 1CC MM: the 1CC of a game and of its special modes, added up. The best score of the game counts, never the last one. In arcade, the credit counter rules: after a continue, the certified score stops at the last score shown before it.

The passport

Each score leaves the machine with a signed passport (ECDSA P-256, canonical JCS form): it names the emulator, the game's measurement definition, the settings and the game, with their fingerprints. One byte changed afterwards invalidates the signature. The site checks it before publishing, and a public verifier lets anyone redo the check.

Anchoring on a blockchain

Each ranked score enters a Merkle tree whose root is anchored on a blockchain through OpenTimestamps. Anyone can check, without Nelfe Play, that a piece of data existed before a block. Anchoring proves a record was not backdated; it does not prove it is true.

Games and ROMs

A game opens its leaderboard once its measurement has been qualified in the lab and the machine's official .MEM file matches the published profile. Only clean official dumps are accepted: hacks, pirate copies, translations, re-releases and compilations are excluded. A proven PAL version is recognised; its separate leaderboard is not online yet. Nelfe Play distributes no game file: everyone plays with their own copies.

Pending, refused, flagged

A score played with an emulator the platform does not recognise yet is kept pending, with its proof, and enters the leaderboard as soon as the emulator is recognised. Cheats, rewinds, fast forwards, state loads and impossible inputs get a score refused, with the reason shown on the player's account. A macro is flagged and never ranked.

What certification says, and its limits

Certified means: measured by an approved measuring program, checked by public rules, signed and anchored. The signature proves the passport comes from that machine and was not altered; reading the game relies on that approved program, whose code is not public. Recent scores have a replay; the oldest do not all have one.

Every statement on this page comes from the published code of Nelfe Play 1.9.171, the public verification protocol and the live site.

FAQ

What makes a score certified?

A passport signed by the machine, naming the emulator, the measurement, the settings and the game; a check by the site before publication; an anchoring of the log on a blockchain.

Can a cheater get through?

No system is tamper-proof. Cheats, rewinds, fast forwards, state loads and impossible inputs get a score refused; a macro is flagged. The measurement relies on an approved program whose code is not public.

What does the blockchain anchoring prove?

That a piece of data existed before a given block: a record cannot be backdated, and a removal is timestamped too. It does not prove the score is true.

How many games are open?

30 games so far.

Do I have to buy the games from Nelfe Play?

No. Nelfe Play distributes no game file: everyone plays with their own legally acquired copies.

Is any ROM accepted?

No: a clean official dump, of the reference version of the game or of another proven official version.

What if my emulator is not recognised?

The score is kept pending with its proof and enters the leaderboard when the emulator is recognised. After three months without recognition, it stays on the account without being ranked.

Can I watch a game again?

Yes for recent scores: their replay plays on your own machine, with your own copy of the game.

What do I need to take part?

A Windows PC with RetroBat and Nelfe Play installed, an account linked to the machine, and the game as a clean official dump, launched from the NELFEPLAY WORLD SCORING collection.

챌린지

  • 1CC (One-Credit Challenge): 게임의 한 모드를 크레딧 하나로. ✓1CC (One-Credit Clear): 그 크레딧 하나로 게임을 클리어.
  • 1LC (One-Life Challenge): 처음부터 끝까지 목숨 하나로.
  • 1CC MULTI (One-Credit Challenge, multiplayer): 같은 판에서 여러 명이 각자 크레딧 하나로.
  • 1LC MULTI (One-Life Challenge, multiplayer): 같은 판에서 여러 명이, 각자 첫 목숨을 잃기 전까지의 점수.
  • 1CC MM (One-Credit Challenge, Multi Modes): 각 게임의 1CC와 특수 모드의 합계.
  • SPEEDRUN: 종목(스테이지, 보스, 게임 전체)마다 시간 순위가 따로 있습니다.
  • CHALLENGE: 정해진 규칙(무기 하나, 제약 하나)으로 처음부터 끝까지 도전하는 종목으로, 각각 시간 순위가 따로 있습니다. 챌린지는 인증되지 않습니다.
  • Mass Run: 라이브 레이스. 수십 명의 플레이어가 같은 스테이지를 같은 출발점에서 달립니다. 게임마다 형식별 레이팅이 있고, 게임별 Mass Speedrun 상설 랭킹의 상위 10명이 포인트를 얻습니다. 레이스는 인증되지 않습니다.
