What Nelfe Play is designed to bring

01

A storefront that explains the game

Platform, edition, licence and ways to play stay understandable to non-specialists.

02

A documented technical profile

Native features and tested environments are separated instead of collapsed into vague badges.

03

A route to international players

English-first commercial presentation with French support and more languages to follow.

04

A connected future, when relevant

Scores, challenges and events can be described without making them a requirement for publication.

FOUNDING SELECTION

What can be considered

  • ✓New homebrew for classic systems
  • ✓Modern neo-retro games
  • ✓Authorised reissues of existing games
  • ✓Restored or completed unreleased titles
  • ✓Games offered free, paid or pay-what-you-want

A selective, human review

  1. 01

    Initial proposal

    Tell us what the game is, who controls its distribution and where it can be seen.

  2. 02

    Rights and product review

    We clarify authority, territories, files, media, price model and product support.

  3. 03

    Playability Passport

    We document target hardware, native capabilities, test evidence and installation options.

  4. 04

    Distribution agreement

    Nothing is published until the commercial and rights framework is signed.
