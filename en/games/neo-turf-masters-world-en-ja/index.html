The story of the game

The why and the how

Neo Turf Masters is first of all a 1996 golf game, developed by Nazca Corporation and published by SNK on Neo Geo. Nazca, which would go on to create Metal Slug, signed one of its first two games with it before its acquisition by SNK. The Neo Geo Pocket Color version came out in Japan on 29 July 1999, in North America on 31 July and in Europe on 1 October, developed by Saurus. Takashi Nishiyama and Y. Kohdoh produce the game, H. Yamada programs it and Takushi Hiyamuta composes the music. The game resembles Irem's Major Title 2: Tournament Leader, because it was programmed by former Irem employees who had already worked on that title. The NGPC version offers more cartoon-like, less detailed graphics than the original.

What makes it unique

It is a fast, arcade-style golf game. Two modes are offered: stroke play for one or two players, and match play for two. Four fictional courses, located in the United States, Japan, Australia and Germany, host six golfers with different characteristics. Unlike the golf games of the time, one click sets the power, a second the height, and the spin is chosen beforehand with the B and C buttons.

Its influence

The original was re-released on PS2, Wii and PSP in SNK Arcade Classics Vol. 1, on the Wii Virtual Console, then on mobile in 2016 by DotEmu. Hamster brought it into its ACA Neo Geo series under the title Big Tournament Golf, because of a trademark conflict with Augusta National Golf Club. The portable version was re-released in 2021.

Challenges

  • 1CC (One-Credit Challenge): a single credit, in one of the modes of the game. ✓1CC (One-Credit Clear): the game finished on that credit.
  • 1LC (One-Life Challenge): a single life, from start to finish.
  • 1CC MULTI (One-Credit Challenge, multiplayer): a single credit each, several players in the same run.
  • 1LC MULTI (One-Life Challenge, multiplayer): each player's score up to their first life lost, several players in the same run.
  • 1CC MM (One-Credit Challenge, Multi Modes): the 1CC of each game and its special modes, added together.
  • SPEEDRUN: each trial (a level, a boss, the whole game) has its own ranking by time.
  • CHALLENGE: a challenge with set rules (one weapon, one constraint), from start to finish, each with its own ranking by time. Challenges are not certified.
  • Mass Run: live races, dozens of players on the same level from the same start. Each game has its rating per format; the top ten of each game’s permanent Mass Speedrun leaderboard score points. Races are not certified.
