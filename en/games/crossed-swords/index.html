The story of the game

The why and the how

Crossed Swords is an action role-playing game developed by Alpha Denshi and published by SNK in 1991 for Neo Geo cabinets and console. Its principle is close to The Super Spy, released the year before, but with sword fights in place of shooting and punching. In September 1991, Game Machine ranked it tenth in Japan, and RePlay fifth in the United States. A sequel, Crossed Swords II, came out in Japan on 2 May 1995 on Neo Geo CD, one of the few games designed for that format.

How it was made

Alpha Denshi develops Crossed Swords, which SNK published on 25 July 1991 in Japan. Akira Ushizawa produces, Hatsue Honbe, Hiroyuki Toda and Katsue Matsuzaki design, Eiji Fukatsu, Hideo Kamoda and Makio Chiba program. The game recalls SNK's The Super Spy, released in 1990, but replaces shooting and punching with sword fights. The characters are only wireframe silhouettes on screen, as in Punch-Out!!. It is the second attempt at an action RPG on Neo Geo, after Riding Hero.

What makes it unique

The game is played by two on the same screen. Only the characters' silhouettes appear, as wireframe models like in Punch-Out!!, which gives a view halfway between first and third person. The principle is to defend before striking: you wield a weapon and a shield, move to the sides, and guard the upper or middle of the body. There are two weapon strikes, the normal attack and the thrust, and you can turn to magic. Several paths are possible, and you can freely skip the first two chapters. The Voyage Knight crosses the village of Dio, Pulista castle, Matius tower and Gauda fortress to rescue a princess kidnapped by a black knight.

Good to know

On speedrun.com, you find Any%, Any% 1cc and Any% Chapter 3 Start (1CC). The game rewards good timing and defense before attack: you hold a weapon and a shield, move sideways and guard the upper or middle of the body. Several paths exist in the game, and the player can freely skip the first two chapters, which opens shorter routes. A normal attack, a thrust and the magic tied to the weapon round out the options.

Its influence

The game has been cited as a source of inspiration for Rage of the Gladiator, a first-person fighting game released in 2010. It was generally well received by the press, which compared its viewpoint to The Super Spy, Dynamite Duke or Operation Wolf. Hamster Corporation re-released it in its ACA Neo Geo collection on PlayStation 4, Xbox One, Nintendo Switch, Windows, Android and iOS.

  • 1LC (One-Life Challenge): a single life, from start to finish.
  • 1CC MULTI (One-Credit Challenge, multiplayer): a single credit each, several players in the same run.
  • 1LC MULTI (One-Life Challenge, multiplayer): each player's score up to their first life lost, several players in the same run.
