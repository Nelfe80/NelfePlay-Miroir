What is a gameroom?

A gameroom is the waiting room of an online game. It opens when you hold A on a game of the Multiplayer collection: you are placed in it at once, ready. Other players take the free seats, and the game starts for everyone as soon as at least two humans are there.

Nelfe Play picks the core, the options and, for most games, the starting point: everyone plays the same game.

The story

Joining a game

In the RetroBat Multiplayer collection, hold A on a game: the panel opens and places you in a gameroom. FOR PLAYERS… is open to everyone. FOR FRIENDS is kept for your friends, meaning players who follow you and whom you follow. Your selection keeps up to three games waiting.

The start

The game starts as soon as at least two humans are there. The countdown lasts 5 seconds for two players, 15 seconds beyond, 5 seconds when the room is full; in a game with a character choice, each arrival adds 10 seconds. Nobody joins or leaves in the last 5 seconds.

The network

Nelfe Play relies on RetroArch netplay. The platform elects the best-connected machine as host, launches the game and lets each guest in at their seat: seat 1 plays with controller 1, and so on. RetroAchievements are turned off for the session. After each game, the machine records the real ping.

The rating

On 37 games, Nelfe Play reads the end of the match and updates an ELO rating: start at 1,000, factor 32, provisional rating during the first 10 matches, then a letter from E to S. With more players and in teams, the rating follows the OpenSkill model, brought to the same scale. Each player has a rating per game, one per genre, and the ELOF, which sums up their versatility. Only humans are ranked. The other games are played as Classic, with no rating.

Co-op

On four co-op games (Double Dragon, Altered Beast, Bubble Bobble and 19XX), each seat also competes for 1CC MULTI and 1LC MULTI: one player's score on a single credit, or on their first life, while playing together.

Fair play

One minute without input removes a player from their gamerooms. On the third quit, Multiplayer closes for 15 minutes for them, then 15 more at each quit; each match played to the end clears one. A ranked match is voided when cheating is detected at the recap.

The end screen

At the end, the screen shows the players and the duration. Holding A restarts the same match; otherwise, back to RetroBat after 12 seconds.

FAQ

How many games?

93 games in the catalogue, 58 of them arcade, from 2 to 5 players depending on the game.

How do you join a game?

By holding A on a game of the RetroBat Multiplayer collection. The panel places the player in a gameroom and the countdown starts when enough players are there.

Can you play without online friends?

Yes. The public FOR PLAYERS… gameroom waits for an opponent. FOR FRIENDS is kept for players who follow each other.

Can you start a game alone?

No. At least one other human player is needed.

Is it certified like World Scoring?

No. Multiplayer gives an ELO rating, computed on matches whose ending is read. Certified scores, with their signed passport, are those of World Scoring.

Are all games ranked?

37 games out of 93. The others are played as Classic, with no rating.

Which network technology?

RetroArch netplay. Nelfe Play picks the core, the options, the starting point and the controllers, and elects the best-connected host machine. No latency is guaranteed.

Does the Player Deck show in Multiplayer?

No. Multiplayer games keep their own interface, with the player panels.

What do I need to play?

A Windows PC with RetroBat and Nelfe Play installed, a Nelfe Play account linked to the machine, and an Internet connection.

Challenges

  • 1CC (One-Credit Challenge): a single credit, in one of the modes of the game. ✓1CC (One-Credit Clear): the game finished on that credit.
  • 1LC (One-Life Challenge): a single life, from start to finish.
  • 1CC MULTI (One-Credit Challenge, multiplayer): a single credit each, several players in the same run.
  • 1LC MULTI (One-Life Challenge, multiplayer): each player's score up to their first life lost, several players in the same run.
  • 1CC MM (One-Credit Challenge, Multi Modes): the 1CC of each game and its special modes, added together.
  • SPEEDRUN: each trial (a level, a boss, the whole game) has its own ranking by time.
  • CHALLENGE: a challenge with set rules (one weapon, one constraint), from start to finish, each with its own ranking by time. Challenges are not certified.
  • Mass Run: live races, dozens of players on the same level from the same start. Each game has its rating per format; the top ten of each game’s permanent Mass Speedrun leaderboard score points. Races are not certified.
