The 30-second version

What is an adventure?

An adventure is a long game you play with friends over several evenings: one game, one group, one shared save. It starts from the NelfePlay Adventure collection on your cabinet and it keeps going from one session to the next.

There is no score and no ranking here. Adventure is for playing together, not for competing.

    With friendsYou create the adventure and invite your friends. It is exclusive (the people invited) or open (anyone, in turn).
  2. 2

The story

Create an adventure

On your cabinet, open the NelfePlay Adventure collection and hold A on a game: that opens a draft, a waiting room where you invite your friends. A draft is not an adventure yet. It becomes one the first time you really start playing with at least two players; it then shows up in My adventures under an automatic name. A draft left alone is dropped after a day.

Join an adventure

An invitation reaches you as a notification and at the top of the ADVENTURE tab of the cabinet panel: press A to join. To resume an adventure you take part in, pick it in the list with A. When a participant is already waiting, the panel says so ("someone is waiting for you: A to join"). Opening the panel never throws you into a game by itself.

What you need

A Nelfe Play account linked to your cabinet. To invite each other, you must be friends on the site. Everyone needs exactly the same version of the game as the creator, or the adventure appears greyed out with the reason. For the voice room: a linked Discord account, your agreement for voice, and the "open microphone" option on your account page.

During the game

You all play the same game over the network. A gamepad shortcut mutes and unmutes your microphone. You can change the number of players expected from the panel. Games that need an accessory are set up by the cabinet itself: the Multitap for Secret of Mana, the Team Player for The Lost Vikings on Mega Drive. In Secret of Mana, players 2 and 3 press START when their character joins the team.

The shared save

The host saves for the group: every 5 minutes, after each save made by the game itself, and at the end of the session. Each save is kept as a version of the adventure. Your own game saves are never touched.

Resume where you stopped

Next time, the adventure starts again from its last version, with the same friends or others. If a seat is empty, the game waits for someone. With a different number of players, it resumes from the game's own save instead.

Each in their own language

Each player plays in their own language when the game allows it. On Game Boy Advance each console has its own language; on shared-screen consoles the game runs in one language for everyone. The cabinet only shows the versions you own in your language, and the adventure keeps the version it was created with.

Everything on this page comes from the NelfePlay Adventure code and the Data Pack catalogue. Some details, such as joining from the panel, depend on the version of the cabinet software you run.

FAQ

Why do I not see my friend's adventure?

You must be friends on the site, and your cabinet must be linked to your account. An exclusive adventure only shows to the people invited; an open one shows to everyone. Check My adventures on your account page too.

Why is an adventure greyed out?

Because your cabinet does not have exactly the same version of the game as the creator, or a seat or a requirement is missing. The reason is written next to it.

What happens to a draft?

A draft is a waiting room, not an adventure. It becomes an adventure when you start playing with at least two players. Alone, it is dropped after a day.

Can I play alone?

No. An adventure exists once you started it with at least two players.

Is there a ranking?

No. No score is certified and no rating moves in Adventure.

Do I need Discord?

Only for the voice room. Link Discord on your account page and agree to voice; the game itself works without it.

What do I need to play?

A Windows PC with RetroBat and Nelfe Play, a Nelfe Play account linked to the machine, friends on the site, the same version of the game, and an Internet connection.

Challenges

  • 1CC (One-Credit Challenge): a single credit, in one of the modes of the game. ✓1CC (One-Credit Clear): the game finished on that credit.
  • 1LC (One-Life Challenge): a single life, from start to finish.
  • 1CC MULTI (One-Credit Challenge, multiplayer): a single credit each, several players in the same run.
  • 1LC MULTI (One-Life Challenge, multiplayer): each player's score up to their first life lost, several players in the same run.
  • 1CC MM (One-Credit Challenge, Multi Modes): the 1CC of each game and its special modes, added together.
  • SPEEDRUN: each trial (a level, a boss, the whole game) has its own ranking by time.
  • CHALLENGE: a challenge with set rules (one weapon, one constraint), from start to finish, each with its own ranking by time. Challenges are not certified.
  • Mass Run: live races, dozens of players on the same level from the same start. Each game has its rating per format; the top ten of each game’s permanent Mass Speedrun leaderboard score points. Races are not certified.
