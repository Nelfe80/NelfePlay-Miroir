Cookies and local storage

Nelfe Play currently uses only strictly necessary or interface-preference storage. No consent banner is shown because no optional tracker is deposited.

Legal notice Privacy Cookies Site terms Terms of sale Submission terms

Session security

The nelfeplay_session cookie protects forms against forgery, carries one-time status messages and limits abuse. It is HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax and Secure on HTTPS. It expires when the browser session ends.

Language preference

The nelfeplay_locale cookie remembers the English or French interface requested by the visitor for up to 180 days. It is used only to provide the expected language.

What is not present

There is currently no advertising cookie, cross-site tracker, social-media pixel, audience analytics script or embedded payment script. If optional tools are introduced later, they will remain blocked until any consent required by law has been obtained.

Your controls

You can delete these cookies in your browser settings. Removing the session cookie may invalidate an open form; removing the language cookie simply resets automatic language selection.
