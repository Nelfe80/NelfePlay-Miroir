Your scores
follow you everywhere

A nickname, an avatar, zero personal data. Check in on any cabinet of an equipped venue by scanning its QR: your records are saved, found again visit after visit, and join the online rankings.

Checking in on a cabinet

  1. 1 1. Scan the cabinet's QR shown on screen or on a sticker
  2. 2 2. One tap on your phone “Welcome!” appears on the cabinet
  3. 3 3. Play - your scores count tap your phone to leave, that's it

No camera needed, no app to install: everything happens in your phone's browser.

Venue challenges

The cabinet announces the challenge

Full-screen game fanart, the objective in big letters (“Most goals in 5 minutes!”), the entry conditions, a countdown - and a giant QR: scan it, the cabinet is yours.

An esport-grade start

The game launches by itself, you press START, your run freezes on the starting line. When everyone is ready: 5-4-3-2-1 inside the game, and everyone starts on the same second.

Your phone lives the race

Your player card shows the challenge live: the objective, “get ready”, the countdown, time remaining - then the game's top 10 at the finish. Push notifications even with the app closed.

Four ways to win

Timer (highest score), race (first to 15 rings), time attack (first to the boss), survival (last one standing) - all measured inside the game's memory, cheat-proof.

A secure world ranking

Your records are signed by the venue and anchored on the blockchain (OpenTimestamps): impossible to fake. You enter a verifiable world ranking - from your venue to the city, the country, the whole planet.

Trophies you earn

A challenge without real opponents never counts, and entry conditions reserve the finals for the regulars. Your crowns, cups and medals carry real weight.

Checking your scores

While you play

Your player card follows the game live on your phone, and the venue screens show the records to beat.

On your account

It is the same account as in the venue: the one the sign-in link opened at the cabinet. Your scores reach it on their own, with their provenance.

Several venues?

Nothing to do: your records follow you from venue to venue, under the same account. A venue can also give anonymous access to anyone who would rather play without an email: a code then attaches those scores the day you open an account.

Stream your games

Your game on YOUR channel

Save your Twitch stream key in the app (encrypted, never shown again). Mid-game, one tap on “Broadcast my game” - and your gameplay goes live on your channel, automatically branded: your name, the game, the venue.

How your scores are protected

Anonymous by design

Your play identity comes down to an identifier and a nickname. That is all the cabinet and the rankings ever see; your email stays attached to your account, away from the scores.

Signed by the venue live

Every score batch is signed with the venue's own cryptographic key before being published: that is the “verified venue” provenance of the rankings - nobody can inject scores in a venue's name.

Blockchain anchoring live

The score journal is anchored via OpenTimestamps (Bitcoin attestation): chained fingerprints, downloadable .ots proofs on the verification page. Anyone can verify a record was never altered after the fact - with nothing personal ever published.
