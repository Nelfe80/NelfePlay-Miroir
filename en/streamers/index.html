How it runs

A challenge, from the first message to the final standings

Four steps, and you get two hours of live without playing a single run.

  1. 01

    You announce the challenge

    The game, the rule, the duration. You pick the command your chat will type.

  2. 02

    Your chat joins

    One command, and the viewer gets a link good for their account, once.

  3. 03

    A test round

    Before the start, every machine proves it reports what it should. Nobody finds out at the end that their run did not count.

  4. 04

    The scores arrive

    The standings move while you commentate, and close at the time you set.

The standings

Standings nobody disputes

A challenge is only worth it if the last-placed entrant accepts the result. Four guarantees make it defensible live.

  • Same game, same definition. The game fingerprint is checked: the scores really are comparable.
  • One account, one entry. The link is personal and single-use. No second accounts.
  • A test round before the start. Every entrant knows their run counts.
  • A reason you can give live. A disqualification comes with its motive, recorded with the decision.

What lives on after the stream

Your challenge keeps working

A page that stays online

Every challenge keeps its address and its standings. Your viewers go back to it, and it brings people to you.

Scores that follow your viewers

A score joins the NelfePlay profile of whoever set it. They find it again, and they come back to defend it.

The same chain as public venues

Your scores are signed and timestamped like a venue's. Your standings carry the same weight as a cabinet record.

A long event that holds

Marathon mode runs up to fourteen days. A three-day event lasts its three days.

Where the chain stands

You read it here rather than discovering a gap mid-stream.

What runs

  • Challenges with a chat command, and single-use personal entry.
  • The test round and each entrant's status.
  • Scores signed, timestamped and publicly verifiable.
  • Marathon mode up to fourteen days.
  • The public page of every challenge, with its standings.

Under development

  • The streamer-side view of verified challenges.
  • Cross standings between venues and home play.
  • Creating a challenge yourself from your own space.
