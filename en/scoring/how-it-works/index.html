How it works

Your games become certified scores, ranked worldwide, with their replay.

See all games Leaderboards Leaderboards explained

Three steps, on your PC or your cabinet

The same experience applies to a Windows computer and to a personal cabinet. No equipped venue is required.

  1. 01

    Create your profile

    An email and a sign-in link. Your nickname becomes your identity in the standings, and it holds your games and your records.

  2. 02

    Get your machine ready

    RetroBat, APIExpose, then linking to your account. The guide picks up where you are and skips what is already done.

  3. 03

    Play and enter the standings

    Your score leaves signed and timestamped. The replay follows, and your world place appears.

Ways to play

Each open game offers one or more of these modes. The Games page filters them, and everything starts from your machine.

Scoring

Your game's score, certified and ranked worldwide. 1CC: clear the game on one credit. 1LC: the best score of the first life. Every score leaves signed, with its replay.

See all games

Speedruns and ghosts

A level, a boss or the whole game, timed to the frame from the same start for everyone. Your record and the world record run alongside you as ghosts. Each event has its own time leaderboard.

See all games

Mass Run

Live races: dozens of players on the same level, from the same start, each on their own machine. The other runners appear on screen in their avatar's colors. Each game has its rating; races are not certified.

See all games

Challenges

A challenge with set rules, such as a single weapon or a constraint, held from start to finish. Each challenge has its own time leaderboard; challenges are not certified.

See all games

Speedruns, Mass Run and challenges start from the Challenges & Speedruns tab in EmulationStation.

Scores signed and anchored forever

Anonymous badge

Your badge carries only an identifier and a nickname. No personal data appears on the cabinet, on the badge or in this ranking.

Signed by the venue

Every batch of scores is signed with the venue’s own key before publication. That is what the “verified venue” mark means.

Anchored on Bitcoin

The score log is timestamped through OpenTimestamps. Anyone can check that a record has not moved afterwards.

Challenges

  • 1CC (One-Credit Challenge): a single credit, in one of the modes of the game. ✓1CC (One-Credit Clear): the game finished on that credit.
  • 1LC (One-Life Challenge): a single life, from start to finish.
  • 1CC MULTI (One-Credit Challenge, multiplayer): a single credit each, several players in the same run.
  • 1LC MULTI (One-Life Challenge, multiplayer): each player's score up to their first life lost, several players in the same run.
  • 1CC MM (One-Credit Challenge, Multi Modes): the 1CC of each game and its special modes, added together.
  • SPEEDRUN: each trial (a level, a boss, the whole game) has its own ranking by time.
  • CHALLENGE: a challenge with set rules (one weapon, one constraint), from start to finish, each with its own ranking by time. Challenges are not certified.
  • Mass Run: live races, dozens of players on the same level from the same start. Each game has its rating per format; the top ten of each game’s permanent Mass Speedrun leaderboard score points. Races are not certified.
