What is a Mass Run?

A Mass Run is a live race: the runners registered in a room start together on the same level, from the same point. Each plays their own game on their machine; a node of the Nelfe Play network exchanges the positions, and everyone sees the others running beside them.

The first to finish wins in Mass Speedrun; the last one alive wins in Mass Survival.

The story

Joining a race

In the RetroBat Challenges & Speedruns collection, hold A on Super Mario Bros. or Sonic, open the MASS RUN tab, pick the race, then Launch. Registration closes two minutes before the GO!; a one-minute waiting room lets the last registered runners in. Launched with a short press, the game stays a free game: nothing is measured.

The start

In the waiting room, the real level runs and everyone can explore it. Then comes the countdown, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1, GO!, and everyone restarts from the starting point, at the same moment.

During the race

Each player emulates their own game at home; netplay is not used. A node of the Nelfe Play network holds the room and exchanges positions fifteen times per second. The other runners are shown with a slight, smoothed delay. A death sends you back to the start of the level and the timer keeps running; in Mass Survival, it eliminates. Past the room limit, 3 minutes on Super Mario Bros. and 4 on Sonic, the machine closes the game.

The recap

At the finish, a screen shows your time, your place and the ranking around you, updated while the race lasts. Holding A registers you for the next race.

The rating

Each player has a rating per game and per format, computed with the OpenSkill model. A race counts when at least two humans take part; ghosts and simulated runners never count. The rating stays provisional until 5 races. In the general ranking, the top ten of each game's Mass Speedrun score points, from 25 down to 1.

A signed race, not certified

A Mass Run race has no replay: it is not certified and does not enter World Scoring. Its result is signed by the node holding the room, and each runner carries a ticket signed by their machine. A quit, a timeout or a disconnection ranks the runner DNF, in last place.

What opens next

Mass Score is planned, with no rating for now. Other speedrun games are prepared in the code and not yet open to rooms. Watching a race as a spectator is not possible yet.

FAQ

Is it open?

Yes. A node of the Nelfe Play network opens a room every two minutes, every day, on Super Mario Bros. and Sonic.

Which games?

Super Mario Bros. and Sonic The Hedgehog. Other speedrun games are prepared, not yet open.

Which formats?

Mass Speedrun, the first to finish wins; Mass Survival, the last one alive wins. Mass Score is planned.

Is it netplay?

No. Each player emulates their own game at home; only positions go through the node holding the room.

Is it certified?

No. A race has no replay and does not enter World Scoring. Its result is signed by the node, and each runner by their machine.

Do I need my own server?

No. The room is held by a node of the Nelfe Play network.

Can I choose my colour?

A runner's colour is the one of their Nelfe Play avatar.

Can I watch a race?

Not yet: watching a race live as a spectator is not published.

What do I need to race?

A Windows PC with RetroBat and an up-to-date Nelfe Play, an account linked to the machine, the reference ROM of the game and an Internet connection.

挑战

  • 1CC（One-Credit Challenge）：游戏的任一模式，只用一个币。✓1CC（One-Credit Clear）：用这一个币打通游戏。
  • 1LC（One-Life Challenge）：从头到尾只用一条命。
  • 1CC MULTI（One-Credit Challenge, multiplayer）：多人同局，每人一币。
  • 1LC MULTI（One-Life Challenge, multiplayer）：多人同局，每人第一次失去生命前的得分。
  • 1CC MM（One-Credit Challenge, Multi Modes）：每款游戏的 1CC 与特殊模式合计。
  • SPEEDRUN：每个项目（一个关卡、一个首领、整个游戏）都有自己的时间排名。
  • CHALLENGE：按规定规则（一种武器、一项限制）从起点到终点的挑战，每项都有自己的时间排名。 挑战不经认证。
  • Mass Run：直播竞速，数十名玩家在同一关卡从同一起点出发。每款游戏按赛制各有等级分；每款游戏 Mass Speedrun 常设排行榜的前十名获得积分。竞速不经认证。
