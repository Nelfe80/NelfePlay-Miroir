What is a ghost?

A ghost is the exact replay of a recorded time. Nelfe Play runs a copy of the emulator that replays, from the same start, the inputs of that time, and draws its character over your picture, in the colour of its role.

The world record ghost is gold, your record silver. You see where you stand against them at every moment of the run.

The story

One trial, one shared start

A trial is a stage, a boss or the whole game. Stage and boss trials start from an official starting state, checked against its fingerprint; full games start from the game's boot. Everyone runs the same race.

The time

The official time is the machine's, counted frame by frame, from the moment the game hands control to the player until the end event. During an attempt: no pause, no save state, no rewind, no fast forward, no cheat. Each player's best time counts; on a tie, the first to arrive stays ahead.

Certification

A speedrun is certified like a score: core and settings checked, time sent signed. A game that slows down, even slightly, voids the attempt, since time is counted in frames. A trial whose rules change opens a new version, with a fresh leaderboard.

The ghosts

At launch, the trial's leaderboard provides the replay of your record and that of the world record. Each ghost is a full copy of the emulator replaying the inputs of that replay from the start: it follows the original run exactly. It is drawn over the picture, your character staying in front, in gold for the world record, in silver for yours, with the name above. When the world record has no replay, the best time that has one takes its place.

Choosing your ghosts

In the panel, X cycles through the settings: your record and the world record (default), your record only, the world record only, or no ghost. A finish that beats your time replaces your ghost as soon as its replay is ready.

A time is never lost

The ghost emulator only starts if the site recognises its fingerprint. Offline or otherwise, the speedrun starts on the original emulator, without ghosts, and the time counts the same way. This emulator is only used for speedruns and Mass Run, never for World Scoring.

Launching a trial

In the RetroBat Challenges & Speedruns collection, hold A on a game, open the SPEEDRUNS tab, pick the trial, then Launch. Launched with a short press, the game stays a free game: nothing is measured.

FAQ

Is a ghost another player, live?

No. It is the exact replay of a recorded time. Racing live with other players is Mass Run.

Which games?

9 games: Super Mario Bros., Super Mario Bros. 3, Mega Man 2, Super Mario World, Super Metroid, Mega Man X, Sonic The Hedgehog, Sonic The Hedgehog 2 and Rocket Knight Adventures.

How many trials?

116 trials: 105 stages, 5 bosses and 6 full games.

How is time measured?

In game frames, from the end of loading to the end event, with no pause, save state, rewind or fast forward.

Is it certified?

Yes, like a score: core and settings checked, time signed. Challenges are not certified.

Can you choose your ghosts?

Yes: your record and the world record, one of them, or none, with X in the panel.

Does the world record always race with me?

Only when a ranked time with its replay exists for the trial.

Do arcade games have ghosts?

Not today. Ghosts exist on the NES, Super Nintendo and Mega Drive speedrun games.

What do I need to play?

A Windows PC with RetroBat and an up-to-date Nelfe Play, an account linked to the machine, and the reference ROM of the game, whose content is checked.

Los desafíos

  • 1CC (One-Credit Challenge): un solo crédito, en uno de los modos del juego. ✓1CC (One-Credit Clear): el juego terminado con ese crédito.
  • 1LC (One-Life Challenge): una sola vida, de principio a fin.
  • 1CC MULTI (One-Credit Challenge, multiplayer): un solo crédito cada uno, varios jugadores en la misma partida.
  • 1LC MULTI (One-Life Challenge, multiplayer): la puntuación de cada uno hasta su primera vida perdida, varios jugadores en la misma partida.
  • 1CC MM (One-Credit Challenge, Multi Modes): el 1CC de cada juego y sus modos especiales, sumados.
  • SPEEDRUN: cada prueba (un nivel, un jefe, el juego entero) tiene su propia clasificación por tiempo.
  • CHALLENGE: un desafío con reglas impuestas (un arma, una restricción), de principio a fin, cada uno con su propia clasificación por tiempo. Los desafíos no están certificados.
  • Mass Run: carreras en directo, decenas de jugadores en el mismo nivel desde la misma salida. Cada juego tiene su rating por formato; los diez primeros de la clasificación permanente Mass Speedrun de cada juego suman puntos. Las carreras no están certificadas.
